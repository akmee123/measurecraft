Element quantities

Structural / architectural totals used as the basis for materials.

How materials are calculated

Concrete (1:2:4): slabs + columns + beams → cement bags, sand m³, aggregate m³
Plaster (1:5, 10 mm): wall face area → extra cement + sand
Brick: wall face × 101 Nos/m²
Tiles (600×600): slab floor area + 5% waste · adhesive bags
Paint: wall face × 2 faces × 2 coats @ 14 m²/L
