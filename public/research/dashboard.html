Drawings database & AI training

Uploaded plans are stored on the server under data/drawings/ (not in Git by default). Download files here, review them manually, then commit only approved drawings to a private GitHub folder. Human Pro measurements and corrections are the ground truth for improving AI accuracy offline — this app does not auto-train Gemini; use the training export in your own model pipeline.
